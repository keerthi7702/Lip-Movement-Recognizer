## 🚀 Overview

LipRead AI explores the capabilities of artificial intelligence and computer vision to recognize speech by analyzing visible lip movements.

Traditional speech recognition systems primarily rely on audio input. Visual speech recognition, on the other hand, analyzes facial and mouth movements to predict the words or digits a person may be speaking.

The project aims to make visual speech analysis accessible through a user-friendly web interface.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore AI-based visual speech recognition.
- Analyze lip movements to predict spoken content.
- Investigate visual recognition of words and digits.
- Demonstrate the practical applications of computer vision.
- Explore accessibility-focused communication technologies.

## ⚠️ Limitations

Visual speech recognition has inherent challenges:

- Similar-looking mouth movements can correspond to different sounds or words.
- Recognition accuracy may vary with lighting, camera angle, and video quality.
- Occluded mouths and unclear facial movements can reduce performance.
- Speaking speed, pronunciation, and language can affect predictions.
- Short clips may not provide enough visual context for reliable recognition.

**Important:** Lip-reading predictions are not guaranteed to be accurate. They should not be treated as definitive evidence of what someone said.
